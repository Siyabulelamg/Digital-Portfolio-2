## **Section 1: Business Communication**

### Reflection

**Situation:**  
When learning about business communication, I realized that many of my emails and messages lacked professionalism. I often used casual language and didn't consider tone or structure. The chapter explained that formal communication requires correct grammar and avoids slang, while informal allows conversational language.  

**Task:**  
My goal was to learn how to write and format formal business emails that create a professional impression. The module taught me about email structure (subject line, greeting, body, closing, signature) and the importance of matching tone to audience and context.  

**Action:**  
I studied the "Formal vs Informal Tone" section of the Business Communication module and created this professional email writing guide. I learned about the five components of professional emails and practiced rewriting informal messages into formal ones. I applied these skills when creating the example email to Mr. Daniels.  

**Result:**  
I now understand how to structure clear, respectful, and effective business emails. This guide serves as a reference tool I can use whenever I need to write professional emails. The chapter's point that *"90% of workplace friction comes from tone in emails"* really stuck with me, so I now always proofread for tone before sending.

## **Section 2: Interview Skills**

### Reflection

**Situation:**  
Before this module, I had little experience with professional interviews and was often nervous when asked unexpected questions. The chapter explained that interviews are stressful because you're put on the spot, which described my experience perfectly.  

**Task:**  
My task was to learn how to prepare thoroughly for interviews, understand what employers look for, and present myself confidently. The chapter provided a 4-step preparation process and the CAR method for answering behavioral questions.  

**Action:**  
I compiled this checklist from the Interview Skills chapter to help me prepare systematically. I learned the 4 steps: research company, know my "why", create story bank, and rehearse questions. I included the CAR method as a quick reference since the chapter emphasized this technique for behavioral questions.  

**Result:**  
I now feel confident and well-prepared for interviews. The CAR method gives me structure instead of rambling. The 10-step checklist makes interview prep manageable rather than overwhelming. I can now plan ahead, remain calm, and respond clearly.

## **Section 3: Mock Interview**

### Reflection

**Situation:**  
I was required to complete a mock interview to demonstrate professional readiness. Initially, I felt nervous and unprepared for spontaneous questions. Even though I studied the Interview Skills chapter, I hadn't practiced applying the concepts in a realistic scenario.  

**Task:**  
My goal was to practice interview etiquette and improve my confidence in speaking about my skills. I needed to apply the CAR method and demonstrate professional conduct while identifying specific areas for improvement.  

**Action:**  
I created this detailed preparation schedule following the chapter's 4-step process. Each day focused on different aspects: research, question review, CAR preparation, body language practice, full mock interview, self-evaluation, and final refinements. I recorded the Friday mock interview so I could objectively assess my performance.  

**Result:**  
This exercise improved my communication and composure during interviews. Recording myself revealed habits I wasn't aware of, like speaking too fast when nervous. I now know how to evaluate my own performance and continuously refine my presentation skills. The chapter's emphasis on practice eliminating uncertainty was absolutely true.

**Task:**  
I aimed to learn how to network effectively and maintain valuable professional relationships both online and offline. The chapter taught that networking is about building real, ongoing relationships—not just exchanging business cards at formal events.  

**Action:**  
I designed this visual networking strategy map based on the chapter's guidance, organizing it into three branches: online networking, offline networking, and relationship building. I also identified the five key benefits from the chapter and created a practical action plan with immediate and ongoing networking activities. I've already started by updating my LinkedIn profile and joining ICT groups.  

**Result:**  
I now understand that networking is an ongoing process, not a one-time event. The strategy map serves as my roadmap for expanding connections systematically. I've already connected with three former classmates working in tech and researched upcoming tech meetups in Cape Town. The chapter's emphasis on networking as relationship-building rather than transactions has changed my perspective completely.

## **Section 5: Workplace Etiquette**

### Reflection

**Situation:**  
When I started learning about workplace etiquette, I realized professionalism goes beyond technical skill. It's about attitude, respect, and adaptability. The chapter explained that professionalism can advance or end career prospects. I reflected on my own habits and recognized areas for improvement, like sometimes checking my phone during meetings.  

**Task:**  
I wanted to build habits that demonstrate responsibility and a positive work ethic. The chapter broke professionalism into five clear pillars. My task was to understand each pillar and create a practical system for developing these professional habits before entering the workforce.  

**Action:**  
I summarized the 10 main rules from the module into this guide, making them concrete and actionable. I then created a weekly self-improvement plan that focuses on one key behavior each day—punctuality, teamwork, communication, time management, and overall reflection. I included the chapter's 5 pillars as a reference. I've already started implementing this by arriving early to group meetings.  

**Result:**  
I have improved my self-discipline, communication, and consistency. The weekly behavior plan makes professional development manageable with specific daily actions instead of vague goals. I now feel confident entering a professional environment knowing how to behave appropriately. I understand that professionalism is about being intentional and continuously improving, not being perfect.
